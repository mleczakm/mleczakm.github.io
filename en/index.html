PAYMENTS THAT HELP

Fundraising without a platform cut.

BezReszty combines BLIK transfers directly to an organizer’s phone number with automatic matching of bank notifications. Donations reach the organizer’s bank account and the application updates the fundraiser.

Semi-automated verification: phone + mObywatel

The operator calls the submitted phone number. The organizer receives a temporary QR or entry code in their dashboard and shares identity data through mObywatel, Poland’s digital identity app. The operator checks it in the official verifier, records the outcome and makes the decision. The system enforces the steps and re-verification after a phone or bank account change.

Identity, account ownership and the BLIK phone-to-account association are checked separately. Approval is an operator decision, not automated KYC or a guarantee of a fundraiser’s outcome.

GOOD TO KNOW

Before
we begin.

Where should we start with business AI?+

Start with a repetitive workflow and data your team can access. We choose one use case, define how to measure quality and costs, and build a pilot. Its results guide the wider rollout.

Can AI take action in our systems?+

Yes, through purpose-built API or MCP integrations. Depending on the system, this can range from finding information to creating tasks or bookings. We define permissions and which actions need user confirmation.

Can we still edit content after leaving WordPress?+

Yes. We choose an editing workflow that suits the team: a lightweight CMS, content files or WordPress retained only as an editorial backend. The public site can stay static, with forms and dynamic features handled by separate integrations.

Should every WordPress site become static?+

Not every site. Static delivery is a strong fit for business websites, landing pages and content sites. For a complex store or account area, I select a hybrid architecture or a dedicated application after reviewing features, traffic and running costs.
